# Travel and reimbursement
Book travel through the travel desk at least ten working days before you leave. Economy class is the default for flights shorter than six hours; for longer flights, premium economy may be booked with your manager's approval. Hotels must stay within the nightly cap for the city: 180 euros in most cities, and 240 euros in London, New York and Tokyo. While traveling, meals are reimbursed up to 60 euros a day, tips included. File a claim for reimbursement within 30 days of the end of the trip. Keep a receipt for every item above 25 euros.

# Expenses
Submit expenses in the expenses app, one claim per trip or per month. Your manager approves claims within five working days, and approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary. If the app shows error E-4127, it could not read the receipt image: photograph the receipt again in good light, flat and with all four corners visible, then resubmit. Error E-4130 means the date of the expense falls outside an open accounting period, so contact the finance team. Error E-5001 means the cost center has been closed; ask your manager for the new one.

# On-call
Engineers join the on-call rotation after their third month. A shift lasts one week, from Monday at 10:00 to the next Monday at 10:00. The primary engineer acknowledges a page within 15 minutes and the secondary within 30 minutes. Each week on call earns an allowance of 300 euros and one day off in lieu. At the end of a shift, hand over open incidents in writing, with what was tried and what is still unknown.

# Data retention
Customer data is kept for the length of the contract plus 90 days, then deleted from production systems. Backups age out within 35 days. Logs that contain personal data are kept for 30 days, and aggregated metrics without personal data for two years. A customer's request to delete their data is completed within 30 days and confirmed in writing.

# Security
Use the password manager for every work account and turn on two-factor authentication. Never paste customer data, credentials or source code into a tool that the security team has not approved. Report a lost laptop or phone to the security team within one hour. Forward suspected phishing to the security inbox, and never to colleagues.

# Leave
Full-time employees receive 26 days of annual leave plus public holidays. Up to five unused days carry over into the first quarter of the next year. Sick leave needs no doctor's note for the first three days. Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for every parent, and it can be split into two blocks within the child's first year.
