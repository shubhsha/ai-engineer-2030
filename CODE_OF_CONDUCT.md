# Code of conduct

This project is a place to learn, so people arrive here at every level
of experience. We want each of them to leave knowing more than when they
came, and glad that they asked.

## What we expect

- Be patient with questions, including the ones you think are basic.
  Answer the question that was asked, and link to what helped you.
- Criticize code, designs and arguments, never the people who wrote them.
- Assume good intent, and say so when you are unsure what someone meant.
- Give credit for ideas and work, and admit your own mistakes plainly.
- Keep discussions on the topic of the repository and the book.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity,
  background, ability or level of experience.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Reporting

Report behavior that breaks this code privately to the maintainer,
[@shubhsha](https://github.com/shubhsha), through the contact details on
their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the
comment or issue. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens next

The maintainer reviews each report and may respond with a private
warning, by editing or removing content, or with a temporary or
permanent ban from the project's spaces. The response depends on the
harm done and on whether the behavior continues.

This code applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and
discussions, and wherever someone represents the project in public.
